# YouTube Short Script — "What Would You Look Like If You Lived on Mars?"

> Written to mimic the style of **@vid-iaa** — specifically the Mariana Trench short
> (`youtube.com/shorts/JTe8pL9sqvY`). Speculative-biology body-horror, ~50 seconds,
> fast cuts, cinematic and unsettling.

---

## The formula it copies

| Beat | Original (Mariana Trench) | This script (Mars) |
|------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| **Hook** | "How would you look if you lived at the bottom of the Mariana Trench?" | "How would you look if you were born on the surface of Mars?" |
| **Dismiss easy answer** | "Forget about diving suits or submarines." | "Forget about spacesuits or pressurized domes." |
| **Lethal stakes + death** | 1,000× pressure → body "explodes like chalk" | Near-vacuum + cold → body boils and bursts |
| **"Say goodbye to your..."** | Hard skeleton → cartilage, gills, gel skin, fins, huge eyes, needle teeth | Bare skin → armor hide, shrunken lungs, stretched bones, huge eyes, sealed nostrils |
| **Dark punchline** | "Not a Disney mermaid, but this humanoid and terrifying blur." | "Not the heroic astronaut from the movies, but a pale, towering, armor-skinned ghost." |

## Production notes

- **Pacing:** average shot ≈ 2.5s, with one or two **0:01 shock cuts** at the death moment (scenes 4–5) and the eye transformation (scene 13). This staccato rhythm is what the original uses.
- **Color:** lean on the rust-red / pink-tan Martian palette with cold blue shadows, the same way the original used murky blue + warm cabin light.
- **Audio:** low ambient wind drone, a deep sub-bass hit on the "death" cut, and one rising tone under the final reveal.
- **Voice:** keep it measured and clinical — the horror comes from the calm delivery over grotesque visuals.
