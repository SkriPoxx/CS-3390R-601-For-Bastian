# Image Prompts — "What Would You Look Like If You Lived on Mars?"

> Style reference: **@vid-iaa** / Mariana Trench short (`youtube.com/shorts/JTe8pL9sqvY`).
> Dark, cinematic, photoreal speculative-biology body-horror. Each prompt is image-to-video ready.
> Format every render at **9:16 vertical, cinematic, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, film grain**.

---

## Character Bible (keep consistent across all frames)

**THE DYING ASTRONAUT (scenes 1–4):** a human in a worn, torn NASA-style white pressure suit, cracked gold visor, frost spreading across the glass, faint skull visible inside.

**THE MARS-ADAPTED HUMANOID (scenes 5–15):** an extremely tall, elongated humanoid, 2.5m, spindly thin limbs. Skin is **charcoal-black, leathery, plated like overlapping armor scales** across back and shoulders, with **translucent patches** on the torso revealing dark internal organs and a slow heartbeat. **Huge glossy black dome eyes** covering the upper half of the skull. **Thin vertical slit nostrils**, no external ears, hairless. Pressurized, faintly inflated look. Solemn, eerie posture.

**WORLD:** Martian surface — rust-red dust and rock, thin pinkish-tan dusty sky, a small pale distant sun low on the horizon, cold blue shadows, drifting dust and dust devils.

## Tips for generating
- **Lock the character** by pasting the relevant Character Bible block in front of each prompt (or use a reference image from scene 5/7 as image-to-image for scenes 6–15) so the creature stays identical.
- For **image-to-video**, animate subtle motion: drifting dust, slow heartbeat pulse, rising vapor, fluttering fabric, slow camera push-in. Keep moves small — the original holds mostly static frames with one motion accent each.
- Use the **0:12 death** and **0:55 eye** beats as your fastest cuts for retention.
