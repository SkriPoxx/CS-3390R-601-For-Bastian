# Animation Prompts (Image-to-Video) — "What Would You Look Like If You Lived on Mars?"

> Feed each prompt together with the matching frame from `mars-generated-images.md`.
> Style reference: **@vid-iaa** / Mariana Trench short — **mostly locked-off shots, ONE deliberate
> motion accent per clip, slow easing, constant fine particle drift.** Never let the camera fly around;
> the dread comes from restraint. Target **3–5s per clip, 24fps, 9:16**.

## Global motion rules (apply to every clip)
- **One hero motion only** per shot + ambient drift. Resist multiple competing movements.
- **Slow in, slow out** (ease both ends). No constant-speed robotic moves.
- **Camera:** at most a 5–10% push-in or tiny parallax/handheld sway. No orbits, no whip pans.
- **Always-on ambience:** fine red dust drifting, faint volumetric haze, subtle grain shimmer.
- **Negative / avoid:** morphing faces, extra limbs, warping geometry, fast zoom, jump cuts, text, watermark, jitter, flicker, cartoonish motion.

## Per-scene prompts

### 1 · [0:00] Buried helmet + skull
> Very slow 5% push-in on the half-buried helmet. Fine red dust streams across frame left-to-right in the thin wind; a few grains skitter off the visor. The faint skull inside stays perfectly still. Tiny glint shifts on the cracked gold visor as light flickers. Ominous, locked tripod feel, slow ease in and out. Avoid any character motion.

### 2 · [0:03] Skeletal hand lifting helmet
> The gloved skeletal hand slowly raises the helmet a few centimetres; dust sheets off the rim and falls in slow motion. Subtle handheld micro-sway on the camera. Background domes stay static and soft. Single deliberate lift motion, gentle ease, low-gravity falling dust.

### 3 · [0:05] Astronaut on ridge, frosting over
> Hold the frame. Frost visibly creeps and spreads across the cracked visor from the edges inward. Thin vapor wisps escape the suit seams and rise slowly. Suit fabric flutters lightly. Dust devils spin faintly in the far background. Camera dead still; the only fast element is the frost crawling. Tense, eerie.

### 4 · [0:09] Suit ruptures into frozen vapor (DEATH BEAT)
> The empty pressure suit violently bursts in one sharp motion — a rapid outward puff of frozen white vapor and shredded fabric — then everything decelerates into slow-motion drift, particles and cloth settling downward in low gravity. Tiny camera shake on the burst, then settle. This is the one HARD, fast accent in the whole video. Punchy, then slow.

### 5 · [0:13] New humanoid revealed
> Locked shot. The creature stands motionless except for a slow, almost imperceptible breathing rise-and-fall of the chest and a slight skin-darkening shimmer beginning to spread. Red dust drifts past the body. Very slow 4% push-in. Stillness is the point — uncanny, clinical.

### 6 · [0:16] Rubbery pressurized hide
> Slow vertical tilt UP the torso. As the camera rises, the skin visibly tightens and toughens into a matte leathery hide, faintly inflating like it's pressurizing from within. One continuous smooth tilt, no other body movement. Dust particles float through the rim light.

### 7 · [0:22] Skin blackens into armor plates
> Hold the three-quarter back view. The hide darkens wave-like from the spine outward to charcoal-black, and plated scales rise and lock into place across the shoulders and back. Faint radiation sparks streak through the air and ping off the plating. Tiny slow drift-in of camera. Transformation spreads slowly and evenly.

### 8 · [0:27] Translucent back, shrunken lungs
> Locked macro hold. Inside the translucent skin panel, the small withered lungs contract and shrink slightly with a slow, labored breath; ribs flatten inward a touch. Outside, cold dust particles drift by. Only the internal anatomy moves — subtle, wet, organic.

### 9 · [0:32] Heartbeat through skin
> Dead-still camera. A single slow, heavy heartbeat pulses once or twice beneath the translucent skin — the muscle swells and relaxes, wet surface catching the cold light with each beat. Faint ambient dust. Hypnotic, ominous, minimal.

### 10 · [0:34] Towering elongated frame (low angle)
> Slow low-angle push-in / slight crane-up emphasizing the creature's impossible height. The spindly limbs sway almost imperceptibly as if fragile in the wind. Long shadow shifts slightly across the red dust. The small pale sun flares gently behind the head. Awe and dread, very slow ease.

### 11 · [0:41] Swollen straining heart
> Locked close-up. The oversized heart pulses with slow, straining beats beneath the plated skin, each beat visibly harder than the last, skin stretching with the effort. Tight rim light flickers on each pulse. Red dust drifts. Tense, building.

### 12 · [0:44] Eyes balloon into black domes
> The creature's hands press against its face as the eyes rapidly swell outward into enormous glossy black domes in one continuous unsettling expansion. Dust swirls around the head with the motion. Slight camera push-in syncs with the bulge. Second-biggest motion accent of the video — uncanny, smooth, a little fast.

### 13 · [0:48] Macro eye reflecting the sun
> Extreme close-up, almost still. The huge black eye glistens; the reflected tiny sun and red horizon slowly slide across its wet curved surface as the head turns a few degrees. A single slow blink of a nictitating membrane optional. Hyper-detailed, glassy, minimal.

### 14 · [0:52] Slit nostrils filtering dust
> Locked macro. The thin vertical slit nostrils flex open and shut rhythmically, drawing streams of razor-fine red dust inward with each breath; grains caught and swirling at the slits. Shallow focus breathing slightly. Organic, rhythmic, subtle.

### 15 · [0:56] Final reveal — armored ghost striding (END)
> Slow cinematic wide shot. The towering creature strides forward across the plain in slow, deliberate steps, red dust trailing and kicking up behind its long thin legs. Very slow 6% push-in or gentle dolly-follow. The abandoned helmet sits still in the foreground. Small pale sun holds on the horizon. Lonely, epic, final — let it breathe.
